what_this_means: |
  This shows how much gas the average home meter uses in a year, measured in kilowatt hours, or kWh. Most of it is used for heating and hot water.

  Gas gives off carbon dioxide when it burns, so using less is better for the climate and for bills. The best ways to cut it are to stop heat escaping, with loft and wall insulation, and later to swap a gas boiler for a heat pump.

  Gas use changes with the weather and with energy prices, so a cold winter or a price rise can move the figure. Parish figures are estimates made from small neighbourhood areas.

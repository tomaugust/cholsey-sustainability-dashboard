what_this_means: |
  Tree canopy cover is the share of the land that is shaded by the leaves and branches of trees when you look down from above.

  Trees take in carbon dioxide, cool hot streets, soak up heavy rain and give homes to birds, insects and other wildlife. A higher share is better.

  This figure comes from a survey of the whole local ward, so it is an estimate for the parish, not a count of trees. Where the survey only covers one year, the dashboard cannot yet show whether cover is going up or down.

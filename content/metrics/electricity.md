what_this_means: |
  This shows how much electricity the average home meter uses in a year. It is measured in kilowatt hours, or kWh. One kWh runs a one-bar electric heater for an hour.

  Using less electricity saves money and cuts carbon, especially when the grid still burns some gas. A lower figure is better. Moving heating and cars to electricity will push the figure up. So the long-term goal is cleaner electricity, not just less.

  Parish figures are worked out from small neighbourhood areas that only partly match the parish edge, so they are estimates.

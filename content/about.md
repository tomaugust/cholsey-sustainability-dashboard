  - This dashboard shows how Cholsey is doing on trees, green space, home energy and the move to cleaner heating. It compares the parish with nearby parishes, South Oxfordshire and England, and links each measure to things you can do.
  - Every number comes from public open data, and each one can be traced to its source. Select the source line under any figure to see who published it, which area and year it covers, and how it was worked out. Figures marked as estimates have been calculated for the parish from data published for other areas.
  - The dashboard is a community project and is not an official statement by Cholsey Parish Council. The wording on the pages is a draft until it has been reviewed.
  - The code, data and decisions behind the dashboard are open and can be read on GitHub. If you spot a mistake, please open an issue there.

  - heading: Why some figures are estimates
    paragraphs:
      - Public datasets are published for fixed areas such as wards, small neighbourhood areas (called LSOAs) or whole districts. None of them match the Cholsey parish boundary exactly. Where we have to work out a parish figure from another area, we mark it as an estimate and say which area was used.
  - heading: Home energy (electricity and gas)
    paragraphs:
      - The government publishes electricity and gas use for each small neighbourhood area. A neighbourhood area can sit partly inside the parish. We take each neighbourhood area's average use per meter and combine them, weighted by how much of that area lies inside the parish. For Cholsey itself we weight by counts of addresses instead of land area, which is more accurate where homes cluster in one part of an area. This is why parish energy figures are marked as estimates.
      - If any of the neighbourhood areas needed for a year is missing from the published data, we leave that year out rather than fill the gap.
  - heading: Tree canopy cover
    paragraphs:
      - Forest Research publishes canopy cover for each ward. We combine the wards that overlap the parish, weighted by how much of the parish lies in each, and mark the result as an estimate. The England figure only covers the wards in the survey, so it is marked as partial coverage.
  - heading: Accessible green space
    paragraphs:
      - We add up the area of mapped public green space inside each boundary and divide by the number of residents. The map data does not include open countryside or common land, and we leave out allotments, churchyards, cemeteries and golf courses. The result is more likely to be too low than too high.
  - heading: Solar panels and heat pumps
    paragraphs:
      - The Microgeneration Certification Scheme publishes installation counts for the whole district. We apply the district share of homes to Cholsey, so the Cholsey figure is an estimate and cannot show how Cholsey differs from its neighbours. Parish-level counts for neighbouring parishes and for England are not available from this source.
  - heading: How we compare areas
    paragraphs:
      - Each figure is compared with the district and with England using the most recent year available for each. The label reads better, similar or worse, taking into account whether a higher or lower figure is good for that measure. Figures within a small margin of each other count as similar. When the two years differ, the page says so.
  - heading: Missing data
    paragraphs:
      - We never fill gaps by guessing. If a source has no figure for an area or year, the page says that it is not available.

## Decisions
- No new ADR -- this is a direct, mechanical application of ADR-0006's already-accepted "verify by name, don't assume vintage match" method to a new, bigger set of wards, not a new design decision.
- Judgement call: `flag=none` for the district row despite 3 of its 21 wards (Cholsey, Wallingford, Wheatley) using a slightly different area for weighting than Forest Research's own true ward boundary (real overlaps verified at 93.8%-100%, not assumed). Reasoned this through explicitly: the effect size is tiny (Wallingford, the worst case, is only ~0.57% of the district's total area, so even a meaningful error in its own rate barely moves the blended result), and flagging every aggregate with a sub-1%-impact caveat would be disproportionate noise compared to how the project already treats genuinely negligible slivers (dropped silently below `MIN_AREA_WEIGHT` elsewhere). The caveat is documented in the function's docstring for anyone who wants the detail, not hidden.
- Judgement call: scoped this firing to the district row only, leaving the national (England) row for later -- aggregating thousands of English wards (vs. 21) and verifying each one's Forest Research vintage match is a materially bigger task, explicitly logged as needing its own dedicated time rather than being rushed.

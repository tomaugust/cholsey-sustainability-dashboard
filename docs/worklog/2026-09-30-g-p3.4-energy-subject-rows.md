## Decisions
- No new ADR — implementing an already-specified work package (LSOA totals apportioned by address weight is the plan's own stated method), reusing P1.5's already-Accepted weight methodology (ADR-0004), not a new design/scope decision.
- Judgement call: **scoped the postcode-level cross-check out of this pass.** Spec's own wording ("cross-check against a postcode-level sum... where postcode suppression makes the postcode sum incomplete, use the LSOA estimate") assumes a postcode-level sum that's actually parish-specific. But P2.6's `fetch_postcode_energy` only fetches whole-OX10-outcode totals (Wallingford, other neighbouring postcodes, and Cholsey all lumped together) — comparing that to Cholsey's own apportioned figure isn't a real cross-check, just an order-of-magnitude plausibility check at best. A genuine postcode-level cross-check needs a postcode-to-parish mapping (e.g. via ONSPD or NSUL's postcode field, not yet used for this purpose), which is a real, separate piece of work, not attempted this firing rather than doing it half-rigorously.
- Judgement call: scoped this firing to the subject row only (same reasoning as P3.2/P3.3) — comparator rows need each comparator's own LSOA overlap + address weights (7 of the 8 comparators don't have this yet, only Moulsford does via its shared LSOA with Cholsey), a bigger piece of work than reusing an existing weight. District/national are actually more tractable than for P3.2/P3.3, since `fetch_regional_la_energy` (P2.5) already gives DESNZ's own South Oxfordshire/England totals directly — flagged as the easiest next increment in STATUS.md's Next steps.

## Done
- **Avoided the ~40M-row, ~491MB national ONSUD/NSUL UPRN file.** Instead used two much smaller ONS Geoportal lookup tables, found the same way P1.1's boundary layers were (searching the Geoportal's own API rather than assuming a bulk-download file is the only option): "Output Area (2021) to PARNCP to LAD to RGN to CTRY (December 2024) Best Fit Lookup" (each 2021 Output Area assigned to whichever parish holds most of its population) and "Output Area (2021) to LSOA to MSOA to LAD (December 2021) Exact Fit Lookup" (OAs nest inside LSOAs exactly, by construction — no estimation needed for this half).
- Queried live: 13 Output Areas best-fit to Cholsey parish. Looked up which LSOAs those 13 OAs belong to.
- **Finding: THREE LSOAs overlap Cholsey, not the spec's two.** `E01035751` (4/4 OAs, wholly in Cholsey) and `E01028619` (6/6 OAs, wholly in Cholsey) confirm the spec's two. But a third, **`E01035752` ("South Oxfordshire 015I")**, also has OAs best-fitting Cholsey — 3 of its 5 — and wasn't in spec §2 at all. This is exactly what P1.4 was tasked to check ("membership is NOT assumed from the spec's two LSOAs"), so it's logged as a finding, not a `Q-NNN` — there's no ambiguity for Tom to resolve here, just more complete real data to use.
- Cross-checked coherence: the other 2 OAs in `E01035752` best-fit **Moulsford** — one of P1.3's confirmed real comparator parishes, so the split is geographically sensible, not an artefact.
- `pipeline/src/cholsey_pipeline/geography/lsoa_overlap.py`: `find_overlapping_lsoas()`, a pure function (no network) taking the two lookups' raw rows and returning each overlapping LSOA's OA-count share of the subject parish. Deliberately named `oa_count_share`, not "weight" — it's a coarse OA-count approximation, not the real address-count weight P1.5 needs (OAs vary in how many addresses they hold).
- `pipeline/tests/fixtures/lsoa_overlap/`: two small real fixtures (15 rows each) trimmed from live query results. `pipeline/tests/unit/test_lsoa_overlap.py`: 5 tests, including one pinning the three-LSOA result and one specifically for the 3/2 Cholsey/Moulsford split in the third LSOA. 44/44 pipeline tests passing overall.
- `config/geography.yaml`'s `lsoas:` section: added `E01035752`, and an `oa_count_share` field on all three (1.0, 1.0, 0.6), with a comment explicitly warning not to use it as a final apportionment weight.
- `config/sources.yaml`: registered both lookup tables (`ons_oa_to_parish`, `ons_oa_to_lsoa`) with their verified URLs. Noted a minor vintage detail: the OA→parish lookup uses `PARNCP24CD` (2024 parish vintage) while the boundary layers use `PARNCP23CD` (2023) — parish codes are stable identifiers that essentially never change year to year for an established parish like Cholsey, so this is an expected mismatch in dataset vintages, not a real discrepancy (unlike Q-007's actual area mismatch).

## Decisions
- No ADR — this is a factual finding (more complete LSOA membership data), not a scope or method choice with real alternatives to weigh. No Q-NNN either, for the same reason: P1.4's whole point was to not assume the spec's two-LSOA list, so finding a third LSOA is the task succeeding, not a conflict needing arbitration.
- Judgement call: used the OA-level best-fit/exact-fit route rather than downloading the full ONSUD, since P1.4's job is *membership* (which LSOAs overlap at all), not the precise address-count weighting P1.5 needs. This kept the work fast and avoided handling a hundreds-of-MB file for a question that thousands of small lookup rows answer just as well. P1.5 will need to revisit whether UPRN-level ONSUD data is also queryable in the same lightweight way (worth checking before assuming a bulk download is required there too).

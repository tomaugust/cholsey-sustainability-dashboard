## Decisions
- No new ADR -- the `bbox` param is a mechanical extension of an existing fetch function (same pattern `fetch.os_open_greenspace.compute_bbox` already established), and the comparator apportionment method (area weight, since NSUL data isn't available for comparators yet) is already documented reasoning from P1.5/ADR-0004, not a new scope/design choice.

- Judgement call: Moulsford keeps its real address-weighted method (more accurate than area weight) rather than being forced through the new area-weighted comparator path for consistency -- accuracy over uniformity, and the `area_role` param makes this a one-line distinction rather than a special case elsewhere.

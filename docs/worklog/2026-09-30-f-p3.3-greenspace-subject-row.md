## Done
- Checked Open questions first (plan §4.7): Q-005, Q-007, Q-011 still open, nothing new to action.
- **Researched before deciding, rather than guessing** which OS Open Greenspace function types are genuinely publicly accessible: looked up OS's own technical documentation (its inclusion rule for `Playing Field` already excludes school-only enclosed fields; its `Religious Grounds` rule is about on-site natural surface existing, not public access) and found a real, specific critique from the Open Spaces Society (2017, when the dataset launched) that the dataset cannot distinguish a fee-paying private golf course from one with a statutory right of access, and includes allotments/private sports facilities indistinguishably from public ones.
- Wrote **ADR-0008**: accessible types = Public Park Or Garden, Playing Field, Play Space, Other Sports Facility, Amenity - Residential Or Business, Tennis Court, Bowling Green. Excluded = Golf Course, Allotments Or Community Growing Spaces, Religious Grounds, Cemetery.
- **Live-verified the real data**, not assumed: ran `fetch_greenspace_sites` for real (P2.4's existing fetcher) against the actual OS Open Greenspace service — 203 real sites returned near Cholsey + comparators. Clipped to Cholsey's real parish boundary (`data/processed/geography/parishes.geojson`) — 11 real sites intersect it: 2 Playing Field, 3 Play Space, 1 Tennis Court (all accessible, ADR-0008), 3 Allotments, 2 Religious Grounds (both excluded).
- Real accessible area: **89,089.84 m²**. Real parish area (`geom.area` in EPSG:27700, same method P1.5's weights.py uses): 15,896,375.505 m². Real mid-2021 population (already committed, P1.6): 4,404.
  - Value: **20.23 m²/resident**
  - **0.56% of parish area**
- Built `pipeline/src/cholsey_pipeline/metrics/greenspace.py`: `compute_subject_greenspace_row(clipped_sites, parish_area_m2, population, year, ...) -> GreenspaceMetricRow` — a pure function over an already-clipped GeoDataFrame (same fetch/compute split as metric 1). `method=clip` (a direct geometric measurement against the parish's own real boundary, unlike metric 1's ward-rate apportionment). Always `flag=partial_coverage`: OS Open Greenspace itself excludes CRoW open-access country and common land, so this metric systematically undercounts true accessible green space, not just "estimates" the counted types.
- Committed the real clipped fixture (`tests/fixtures/os_open_greenspace/cholsey_clipped_real.gpkg`, the actual 11 sites) and the real download's manifest entry (`data/manifest/os_open_greenspace/...json`) for provenance.

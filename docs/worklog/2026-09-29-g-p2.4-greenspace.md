## Done
- Checked Open questions first (plan §4.7): Q-005 and Q-007 still open, nothing new to action.
- Downloaded the real GB-wide OS Open Greenspace GeoPackage (via the `download_url` verified in P2.1) to inspect its actual structure before writing anything: two layers, `greenspace_site` (165,978 polygons GB-wide) and `access_point` (points, not used here). - Built `pipeline/src/cholsey_pipeline/fetch/os_open_greenspace.py`:
  - `compute_bbox` — pure function, buffers a GeoDataFrame's total bounds (default 500m).
  - `read_greenspace_sites` — thin wrapper over geopandas/pyogrio's bbox-filtered read, same fetch/parse split as every other Phase 1/2 module.
  - `fetch_greenspace_sites` — ties `fetch.http.fetch_file` (P2.2), `compute_bbox`, and the zip-URI read together for the real live fetch.
- New fixture: 10 real greenspace sites near Cholsey (one per distinct `function` value — Play Space, Playing Field, Public Park Or Garden, Allotments, Religious Grounds, Golf Course, Other Sports Facility, Tennis Court, Cemetery, Bowling Green), trimmed from the live bbox-filtered read. Committed as a small `.gpkg` (matching the production format, unlike GeoJSON which doesn't support the `layer=` parameter the same way) rather than GeoJSON.

## Decisions
- No new ADR — straightforward implementation of an already-specified work package (P2.4), no scope/spec/stack judgement calls beyond the buffer distance (500m, documented inline as "generous enough not to cut off a straddling site, small enough to keep the extract tiny").
- Judgement call: committed the test fixture as a small `.gpkg` rather than converting to GeoJSON, since `geopandas.read_file(..., layer=...)` only makes sense against a multi-layer format — a GeoJSON fixture broke the layer-filtered read entirely (confirmed by trying it first) rather than silently reading the wrong thing.
